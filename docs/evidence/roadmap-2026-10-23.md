## 2. Device-adaptation contract before M5 phase 2a

The M5 plan already requires "no GPU-specific constants" for the quantum kernels, and the
earlier backlog items stated the same intent through device attributes; the 3090 check shows
that thresholds measured on one device and expressed through attributes are still
device-specific. To keep M5's new kernels from becoming a later retrofit, a short contract
document (`docs/evidence/device-adaptation-contract.md`) is written **before phase 2a** and
the phase-2a/4 kernels follow it. It states at least:

1. Launch and path decisions come from device attributes, occupancy queries and/or a measured
   calibration on the running device, never from a constant measured on one machine alone.
2. Every fitted threshold is validated on at least two architectures (GB10 sm_121 and RTX 3090
   sm_86) before acceptance; the evidence states the devices.
3. An unvalidated device gets the conservative choice (the path that is never much slower),
   not an extrapolation.
4. Decisions stay deterministic for a given device (summation-order changes are results
   changes, as the port-aarch64 inventory notes).

Writing the contract is not the M7 retrofit: existing rules (C5, the M7 wavelet model, C1-C3
thresholds, workspaces) are changed in M7, not in M5. The known C5 sm_86 regression stays
recorded until then.

## 3. M5 scope amendment

Owner agreement of 2026-10-23 to narrow M5 so that the stages above are reached:

- **Required core:** phase 1 (PQC CPU), phase 2a (PQC resident CUDA), phase 5 (QUBO and
  quadratization), phase 7 (Python and acceptance), and phase 8 (new, below).
- **Fock (phases 3 and 4): behind a go/no-go gate.** Before phase 3: the numerical feasibility
  preflight (review R6) including the norm-deficit criterion (R1), and a small CPU prototype
  compared by time-to-quality with the normalized Hermite-function carrier (backlog M5) on
  one-dimensional tasks. The owner decides at the gate (default if unanswered: no-go, Fock
  recorded as future work with the preflight evidence).
- **Deferred, outside M5 acceptance unless the owner reinstates them:** phase 2b (cuStateVec
  feasibility and matched comparison) and phase 6 (finite shots). Their plan text stays as
  the specification for later.
- **Phase 8 (new): time-to-quality study, measurement only.** PQC against the classical
  carrier with the matching spectrum (a single-qubit Y-encoded circuit with r re-uploads is a
  trigonometric polynomial of degree <= r in the encoded angle, so FourierEdges of that degree
  with matched parameter count), and splines/wavelets on localized targets; protocol from the
  owner-questions document (targets and splits written before runs, equal tuning budgets,
  censored runs reported as failures, FP32, repeated seeds) on GB10. Not a pass/fail gate.
- **GPU runtime validation (Q7 resolved):** an RTX 3090 is available, so every GPU phase
  (2a, and 4 if Fock goes ahead) records runtime parity and tests on sm_86 with MSVC in
  addition to GB10, not only an sm_86 compile.
- **Profiling scope for the experimental carriers (Q11 / AGENTS rule 6), a scope
  clarification, AGENTS.md unchanged:** for PQC/Fock the profiling requirement is met by
  matched complete-step measurements, identification and removal of clearly pathological
  bottlenecks, and compliance with the device-adaptation contract; exhaustive kernel tuning is
  not an acceptance requirement. Conventional carriers keep the full rule.
