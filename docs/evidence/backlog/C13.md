# Backlog C13: FP32/TF32 input VJP of B-spline layers (derivative coefficients)

Date: 2026-10-14. Branch `cpp-foundation`, base `f9b8d01` (C12 closed). Machine: GB10 (sm_121,
48 SMs, 24 MiB L2, unified LPDDR5x), CUDA 13.0, cuBLAS 13.1, GCC 13.3
([port evidence](../port-aarch64.md)). Three Python processes of other users held GPU contexts
and were idle during the timed runs ([gpu-before.txt](C13-bench/gpu-before.txt),
[gpu-after.txt](C13-bench/gpu-after.txt)).

## Scope change (AGENTS rule 5)

C13 is a **new backlog item** added on 2026-10-14 by owner decision on the open question of the
[C5 evidence](C5.md#observations-and-open-questions): the dense TF32 input VJP of large-term
B-spline layers was grossly wrong in C5's two-layer case (0.398 expected from the FP64 CPU,
-0.060 computed). Priority P1, table C. The backlog grows from 28 to 29 items. Owner-approved
plan: diagnose; derivative via coefficient differences (Option 2) for FP32/TF32 on the dense
and sparse paths, FP64 unchanged; check other families and use FP32 math for their W GEMM
(Option 1) only where a family fails; contract statement per family; optional path override.

**The cited C5 case is not fixed by C13, and was not caused by the input-VJP formula.** On
the exact C5 data the dense TF32 network input VJP is still -0.062 against 0.398 (base:
-0.060). Its cause is the second layer (degree 1, 30 terms): its derivative jumps at every
knot, and the TF32 forward rounds layer 0's outputs by 2.6e-4 (about `2^-11` of the terms), which
moves some layer-1 inputs across a knot, so the CPU evaluated at *its own* activations takes
the other slope. Against the CPU evaluated at the executor's own layer-1 upstream the base
build was already within the C1 TF32 tolerance (0.38 of it) and C13 halves that (0.17);
the sparse path avoided it only because its forward is FP32
([two-layer-probe.txt](C13-bench/accuracy/two-layer-probe.txt)). The K-amplified rounding
that the plan hypothesised is real and is what C13 fixes (rows above), but it is a separate,
smaller effect at the cited sizes. See "Owner questions".

## Diagnosis (RED)

Harness [dx_accuracy.cpp](C13-bench/accuracy/dx_accuracy.cpp): one KAN layer 64 -> 64, batch
1024, the CPU reference at the executor's (rounded) parameters and FP32-representable inputs
and upstream, so only the executor's arithmetic differs. Coefficient profiles: `wave` (the
C1/C5 suites' `sin(0.731 k)`), `smooth` (smooth over the term index), `decay` (decaying
spectrum), `fit` (least-squares fits of smooth functions `sin(w x + phi)`: a trained layer's
coefficients), `vs` (VarianceScaling). Columns: the C1 tolerance ratio, `amp` (error over
`2^-11 sum_k |Phi'_k| sum_o |U||C_k|`, the W = U*C formula's rounding bound), `diff` (error over
the difference form's bound) and `edge` (error over `2^-11 (sum_o |U||f'_oi(x)| + 1e-2 max)`,
the scale of the true edge derivatives).

- **Mechanism confirmed.** `amp` stays at 0.2-0.4 for every K and profile: the error is the TF32
  rounding of `W = U*C`, amplified by `sum_k |B'_k|`. Forcing only the W GEMM to FP32 math in
  TF32 mode ([w-gemm-fp32-diagnostic.patch](C13-bench/accuracy/w-gemm-fp32-diagnostic.patch),
  `KAN_C13_W_FP32=1`) makes the TF32 dense input VJP identical to FP32's (K=12/48/192, smooth:
  0.0013/0.0057/0.018 -> 3.4e-7/1.5e-6/4.9e-6 absolute).
- **It grows with K.** Smooth coefficients, TF32 dense, C1 tolerance ratio 0.30 (K=6), 0.56 (12),
  0.96 (24), 2.6 (48), 3.3 (96), 7.0 (192); `fit` 1.84 at K=96, 2.56 at 192. FP32 has the same
  growth about 8000x weaker (0.013 -> 0.30), the sparse path the same in FP32 (it computes in
  FP32 under TF32). For rough coefficients (`wave`, `vs`) the true derivative grows with K as
  well and nothing fails ([accuracy-base.csv](C13-bench/accuracy/accuracy-base.csv),
  [summary.md](C13-bench/accuracy/summary.md)).
- **RED test** ([C13-red.log](C13-red.log)): the final suite
  `tests/backends/cuda/local_spline_gradient_resident_test.cpp` linked against the base library.
  The two bound cases fail (error/bound up to 75.6 TF32 dense, 89.3 FP32 dense, 86.3 FP32
  sparse); the two-layer, degree-0 and overflow cases are pinning cases that pass before and
  after. The FP32 RED is clear only at large term counts (259 terms; 1.2-1.3 at 129, the
  32-interval FP32 cases pass on the base); TF32 dense is RED at every size.

## Design

**Identity** (`src/carriers/local/local_formulas.hpp`). `d/dx sum_k c_k B_{k,p} = sum_{j=1}^{K-1}
C*_j B_{j,p-1}` with `C*_j = (c_j - c_{j-1}) s_j`, `s_j = p/(t_{j+p} - t_j)` (zero gap: `s_j = 0`;
gap beyond the range: the halved division of `spline_slope_term`). It is exactly the
regrouping that `spline_evaluate`'s derivative expressions perform per input: `d[r] =
slope(lower[r]) - slope(lower[r+1])` with `lower` the degree-`p-1` values at the start of the
last Cox-de Boor step. New `spline_evaluate<false, true>` (`spline_lower_terms`) writes those
`lower[1..p]` instead of the derivatives, from the same span search and recurrence, so the span,
the side at repeated knots and the right endpoint are the forward's; it still evaluates and
guards the derivative expressions, so the forward status is unchanged. Weights are nonnegative
and sum to one: rounding of `C*` or of `U*C*` is not amplified.

**Resident CUDA** (`src/backends/cuda/resident.cu`; trait `spline_difference_vjp<T>`, false for
double, so FP64 keeps `W = U*C` and `Phi'`):
- Knot scales `s_j` per FP32/TF32 B-spline layer (`terms-1` values after its knots), computed
  on the host in T from the rounded knots at construction (knots are structure).
- `spline_difference_kernel`: `C*` from the current `C` in every backward that needs the input
  VJP (graphs, `upload_parameters` and rollback need nothing), 32-bit indices when
  `edges*terms` fits.
- Dense: `W* = U*C*` (cuBLAS, TF32 tensor ops under TF32, rows of `terms-1` in the W scratch),
  then `backward_finish_kernel<SplineDifferences<T, Degree>>`: per (sample, input)
  `spline_lower_terms` and a gather of `p` values of the W* row (`Degree` 1..3 compile-time, 0 =
  any degree). The C3 Phi' recompute is no longer used by B-spline layers.
- Sparse: the basis kernel stores `P-1` degree-`p-1` rows instead of `P` derivative rows; `C*` in
  the layout of `C` (rows of `terms`, the last zero) staged by the unchanged
  `spline_input_kernel<P-1>`; degree 0: `dx = 0` (fill).
- Jacobi (Option 1, below): `Context::with_fp32_math` around its W GEMM.
- No `Phi'` rows for FP32/TF32 dense B-spline layers at any length (review finding): beyond the
  85-term stage the basis kernel now writes only the values (zero fill plus the `p+1` values of
  `spline_span_terms`, the same operations, so the forward is bitwise unchanged:
  [long-row-forward.txt](C13-bench/golden/long-row-forward.txt), 60/130/300 terms, degrees 3
  and 5, FP32/TF32). Full step ([abba-long-rows-summary.md](C13-bench/abba-long-rows-summary.md)):
  64x64 p5 K=130 FP32 2.97 -> 1.10 ms (arena 109 -> 79 MB), 256-wide p5 K=130 TF32 209.9 ->
  110.8 ms, p3 K=130 FP32 235.4 -> 128.9 ms (arena 7572 -> 4484 MB): the unstaged kernel no
  longer writes the derivative rows with per-thread strided stores.
- `spline_derivative_coefficient` halves an overflowing difference first, so `C*` overflows
  only when its value does (review finding); the 32-bit index form of the difference kernel
  also requires `edges*terms` plus the grid stride to fit (no wrap).

Profiling-driven choices (nsys, [nsys/](C13-bench/nsys/), 256x256x256x10 b8192 TF32 dense):
- The first finish kernel used runtime-degree local arrays: 855 us per launch against the
  base's 605 us (C3 staged recompute + K-term dot) at K=12. With a compile-time degree for
  1..3: 344 us (K=12) and 521 us (K=48, base 2575 us). `spline_difference_kernel`: 9.6 us
  (K=12), 52 us (K=48).
- 1024-wide FP32 sparse K=48 (the largest C*): `C*` in rows of `terms-1` made
  `spline_input_kernel` 1.0 ms slower (15.9 vs 14.9 ms) and with the 1.75 ms difference kernel
  the step 1.04x; computing the differences while staging the C tiles instead was 102-108 ms
  against 92.5 ms (the tiles are staged once per block of samples). Rows of `terms` (the C
  layout) give 91.7-92.3 against 92.5-92.7 ms.

**Selection rule re-measured** (costs shifted): forced dense against forced sparse on the C13
build ([crossover-new.csv](C13-bench/crossover-new.csv)): FP32 256-wide sparse/dense speedup
0.67/0.81/0.98/1.02/1.18 at K/(p+1) = 1.5/2/2.5/3/4 (degree 3), 0.68/0.88/1.09 at 2/3/4
(degree 1); TF32 0.86/0.98/1.12/1.21 at 3/4/5/6, degree 1 0.94 (4), 1.25 (6); 1024-wide FP32
1.03/1.25/1.44 at 4/6/8, TF32 0.93/1.01/1.28 at 16/20/24. (The base build already gave 0.91 at
FP32 K=8: part of the FP32 shift predates C13.) New ratios: FP32 4 (was 2), TF32 5 (was 4),
TF32 out of L2 20 (was 16); FP32 out of L2 6 and all FP64 values unchanged (full-rate FP64
keeps C5's 2/6). Automatic against forced dense at the new boundaries
([rule-check.csv](C13-bench/rule-check.csv)): 0.97-1.23x; the two configurations below 1.00 are
FP32 64-wide K=48 0.97 (one sparse layer by the work floor, 0.99 in C5) and TF32 degree 1 K=10
0.98 (ratio 5; degree 3 at ratio 5 is 1.11). Validated on GB10 only, like C5's.

## Other families (step 3)

Matrix and rule ([option1/](C13-bench/option1/)): every fixed family at sizes 8-256, profiles
wave/smooth/decay/fit/vs, TF32 dense, 64 inputs; then, for the polynomial families that failed
or nearly failed, `fit` and `decay` x sizes 8-64 x inputs 64/256 x sin-spread/uniform inputs.
The decision rule was written down before that matrix ran
([option1-criterion.md](C13-bench/option1/option1-criterion.md)): Option 1 for a family that
exceeds the C1 TF32 tolerance in more than one cell whose forward stays within it.

| family | worst dx / C1 TF32 tolerance (profile) | forward | decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chebyshev 8-64 | 0.89 (wave), 0.74 (decay, fit), 0 of 32 matrix cells fail | <= 0.06 | TF32 |
| Legendre 16-64 | 1.23 (fit, 16, 64 inputs, sin inputs): 1 of 32 cells | <= 0.07 | TF32 |
| Jacobi (0.3, -0.2) 16-64 | 1.08-1.85 (fit): 12 of 32 cells, every size >= 16 | <= 0.12 | **Option 1** |
| Hermite functions 16-256 | 1.11/1.77 only for the synthetic smooth-in-k profile at 128/256; fit 0.16-0.18 | <= 0.16 | TF32 |
| Hermite (physicists) 8 | 3.89 (fit) | 19.8 (fit) | TF32: ill-conditioned representation |
| Fourier 9-129 | 0.36 | <= 0.17 | TF32 |
| Gaussian RBF 8-256 | 0.79 | <= 0.17 | TF32 |
| Mexican hat 16-256 | 0.95 (fit 16); 5.4/21 (fit 64/256) | 2.2/32 (fit 64/256) | TF32: ill-conditioned (zero-mean wavelets fitted to nonzero-mean functions; FP32 fails too, 6.75) |

Option 1 for Jacobi: with FP32 math the fitted cases drop from 1.23-1.70 to 3e-4-5e-4 of the
tolerance (`KAN_C13_W_FP32` diagnostic), and the C13 build reproduces that
([accuracy-new.csv](C13-bench/accuracy/accuracy-new.csv)). Cost, measured on the implementation
([jacobi-abba.csv](C13-bench/option1/jacobi-abba.csv), base vs C13, ABBA): 1.042x of the
256-wide Jacobi step (11.65 -> 12.14 ms), 1.154x at 1024 wide (27.7 -> 32.0 ms); Chebyshev
control 1.004x. (Measured on other families with the diagnostic switch: 1.04-1.06x at 256 and
64 wide, 1.14-1.16x at 1024 wide, [option1-abba.csv](C13-bench/option1/option1-abba.csv).) The
`edge` column (error on the scale of the true derivatives) is larger than 1 for most families
under some profile (polynomials up to 85 for fits, Gaussians up to 40): TF32 rounding of `C`
is amplified by the derivatives of every global family to some degree. Within the C1 tolerance
this is the documented TF32 trade-off, not applied globally; recorded as an owner question.

## FP64 (owner question, measured, not adopted)

A measurement-only build with the trait true for double (`f64v`, FP64 also on the difference
form; [fp64-variant-abba.csv](C13-bench/fp64-variant-abba.csv), ABBA against the C13 build):
FP64 steps 0.937-0.999x (64-wide K=48 dense 0.937, 256-wide K=12 dense 0.966, K=48 auto

change every FP64 result containing an input VJP (golden), so per the plan it stays an owner
question.

## Owner questions

1. **The cited case.** It is a knot jump of a degree-1 layer under TF32 forward rounding, not the
   input-VJP formula; C13 does not change it. If TF32 dense B-spline networks should agree with
   the CPU across such layers, the TF32 forward of B-spline layers would need FP32 math (Option 1
   for the forward GEMM; it would cut the forward error 2.6e-4 by about 8000x and the straddle
   rate with it). Not measured or applied without a decision.
2. **FP64 on the difference form**: 3.5-6% faster steps (above), would change FP64 golden results.
3. **Wider Option 1**: TF32 input-VJP errors of global families on the scale of the true derivative
   (`edge` up to 85 for polynomial fits, 40 for Gaussian fits) mostly stay within the C1
   tolerance; single cells exceed it (Legendre fit 16: 1.23x; Hermite functions with a
   non-decaying profile at 128/256: 1.11x/1.77x), which the fixed rule does not count as a
   family failure, and Jacobi with alpha = beta = 0 is Legendre. Option 1 for them would cost
   1.04-1.16x of the step. Separately, the FP32 input VJP of the 256-center Mexican-hat fit
   exceeds the C1 FP32 tolerance (6.7x, pre-existing, ill-conditioned representation).
4. **Rule constants**: FP32 4, TF32 5/20 are GB10 measurements of complete steps, like C5's.
